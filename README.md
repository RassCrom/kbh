# Kazakhstan Building History

## About the Project (Entry Form)

### Project Goal
Visualization of the building history and development of Kazakhstan's cities on an interactive map. The project allows users to explore how the appearance of cities has changed over time, study the architectural heritage, and the chronology of buildings' construction.

### Target Audience
- Urban planners, architects, and researchers of the urban environment.
- Historians and local history experts studying the development of settlements.
- Residents and tourists interested in the history of their city or neighborhood.
- Students of related specialties.

### Technology Stack
- **Frontend Framework:** React
- **Mapping:** MapLibre GL
- **Programming Language:** TypeScript
- **Bundler:** Vite
